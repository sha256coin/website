Privacy Policy

S256 does not collect, store, or process any personal data. Transactions in the Web Wallet and Android Wallet are signed locally on your device; your seed phrases or private keys are never transmitted to any server. We do not use tracking cookies. Server logs may temporarily collect IP addresses strictly for infrastructure security and DDoS prevention. Both wallets are designed as fully noncustodial solutions where users maintain complete control and ownership of their funds.
